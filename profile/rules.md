# My rules

Personal rules every skill must follow, on top of the kit's generic rules. `resume-learn` adds a
line here whenever you correct Claude, so you never give the same feedback twice. Newest last.
Format: `- <rule> (why) — <YYYY-MM-DD>`

## Resume
- <e.g. Skills line leads with the skills this JD asks for first.>

## Outreach
- <e.g. Never more than 5 sentences in a cold LinkedIn message.>

## Naming (what to call things)
- <e.g. Call the internal tool "the pricing engine", never its code name.>

## Interview
- <e.g. In a mock, give feedback after every answer, not at the end.>
